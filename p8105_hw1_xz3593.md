The mean only works for `random_sample` and `is_positive`, it only works
for numerical and logical variables.The mean does not work for
`characters` and `category`.Because character and factor variables
cannot be averaged directly.

``` r
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, is_positive))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, characters))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, category))
```

as.numeric() turns TRUE into 1 and FALSE into 0, which can explain why
the logical variable has a mean of 0.4. The letters become NA because
they are not numbers. The factor values become 1, 2 and 3, but these are
just representation for A, B, and C, so their average would not be
meaningful.
